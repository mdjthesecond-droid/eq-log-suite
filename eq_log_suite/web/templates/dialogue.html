Classic EQ has no logged "quest completed" or "give item to NPC" line at all -- only NPC dialogue, your own spoken responses, and whatever you're handed back. This is a chronological transcript of those, per character, so a real dialogue tree emerges from repeated visits to the same NPC over time. Bracketed [keywords] in an NPC's line are the conversation's actual branch points -- echo one back with /say to continue. zone is whatever zone_change happened most recently before the line; npc is who you hailed (for a hail line) or who's speaking (for dialogue/reward lines) -- blank for a plain "you say" line, since text alone can't tell who you were talking to.
