Quests -- {{ game_label }}

Classic EQ almost never logs a "quest completed" or "give item to NPC" line -- only NPC dialogue, your own spoken responses, whatever you're handed back, and (rarely -- confirmed real but only once in a 425k-line log) an actual item hand-in ("You offered ... to ...") and its completion. This is a chronological transcript of all of that, per character, so a real dialogue tree emerges from repeated visits to the same NPC over time. Bracketed [keywords] in an NPC's line are the conversation's actual branch points -- echo one back with /say to continue. Pure ambient chatter (no bracketed keyword, no item exchange) is hidden here -- it's not lost, just moved to that NPC's own page, so this stays focused on what's actually quest-relevant. zone is whatever zone_change happened most recently before the line; npc is who you hailed (for a hail line) or who's speaking/ receiving (for dialogue/reward/offer lines) -- blank for a plain "you say" line, since text alone can't tell who you were talking to.
