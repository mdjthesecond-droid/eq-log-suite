Neither the assignment nor the reward line names an NPC or zone, so both are correlated: zone is whatever zone_change happened most recently before the assignment; npc is whichever NPC most recently spoke before it (best-effort -- not a guaranteed source, just whoever was talking right before). A blank reward means no matching task_reward has been seen yet for that task name -- some sub-tasks grant their reward under a different name than they were assigned under, so a real reward can still fail to show up here.
