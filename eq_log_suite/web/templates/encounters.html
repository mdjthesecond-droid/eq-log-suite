Encounters

Full encounter history (not just the live session) -- filter by character and/or a date range to work through a whole day or an entire imported log. See Live for the real-time "active encounter" view of the last 40. Each encounter is named after the first mob you engaged, since same-named mobs can't be told apart from log text alone, "mob" here just means "whichever name is on the other end of a combat line" -- so a duo partner's name can occasionally show up in the mob list too.
