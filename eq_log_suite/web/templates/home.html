EQ Log Suite

Historical analysis over your parsed EverQuest logs -- trends, drop rates, encounter breakdowns, whatever the point-and-click filters don't cover via raw SQL. For real-time monitoring while you're actually playing, see Live instead.

  • Encounters -- full encounter history with character/date filters, for working through a whole day or an imported log.
  • Browse Events with point-and-click filters (source, target, verb, outcome, time range).
  • SQL Query for anything the filters don't cover -- raw SELECT against the DB.
